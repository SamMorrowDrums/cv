My team and I built a thing: [GitHub Copilot Connectors](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/customizations/copilot-connectors)

Check out my demo at GitHub Universe, you can try connectors out immediately via [copilot/customize](https://github.com/copilot/customize) (make sure you log in first). This has been a huge improvement in convenience, especially the fact the GitHub iOS app now lets me use my connectors in Copilot Chat.

Universe itself was absolutely amazing to attend, so many people are building such amazing things - and it's an incredible production too!

I worked at the booth on day 2 which was a full sized diner, kitchen and speakeasy!
